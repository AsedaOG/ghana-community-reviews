# Ghana Community Review Platform

## Vision

A trusted, anonymous review platform for Ghana where people can share experiences about places they live, work, study, and receive services.

The platform focuses on transparency and informed decision-making by allowing users to review:

- Apartments
- Landlords
- Workplaces
- Schools
- Hospitals
- Gyms

Users remain anonymous through automatically generated usernames, while businesses and property owners can publicly respond to reviews.

## Mission

To help Ghanaians make better decisions through authentic community-driven reviews and ratings.

## Core Categories

- Apartments & Landlords
- Workplaces
- Schools (Kindergarten, Primary, JHS)
- Hospitals
- Gyms

## User Roles

### Anonymous Reviewer
- Generated username
- Submit reviews
- Upload evidence
- Upload photos (except workplaces)
- Earn badges and reputation

### Business Owner
- Claim business profile
- Respond to reviews
- Manage profile information

### Administrator
- Create listings
- Verify evidence
- Moderate content
- Handle disputes

## Geographic Structure

Ghana → Region → District → Area

## Review Verification Levels

1. Community Review
2. Verified Experience
3. Trusted Reviewer

## Revenue Model

### Phase 1
Free platform focused on growth.

### Phase 2
Business subscriptions and profile management.

### Phase 3
Premium market intelligence reports.

## Technical Stack

- Frontend: Next.js
- Backend: Django REST Framework
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Storage: Cloudflare R2 / AWS S3
- Search: PostgreSQL Full Text Search
